Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026.

Content HQ is a private dashboard for one creator's own TikTok accounts.

  • What we read: after you sign in with TikTok, your username, display name, avatar, follower, following and like counts, and view, like, comment and share counts for your videos.
  • What we store: those numbers and the TikTok access tokens, kept in private Cloudflare storage used only by this dashboard. We never see or store your TikTok password.
  • Sharing: nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
  • Deleting: choose Disconnect in the dashboard's account menu, or remove the app in TikTok's settings. Disconnecting revokes the tokens and deletes the stored data for that account.
