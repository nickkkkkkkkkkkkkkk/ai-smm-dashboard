Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026.

Content HQ is a private dashboard for one creator's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

  • What we read: after you sign in, your account name, avatar and follower or subscriber counts, and the view, like, comment and share counts of your own videos. With YouTube this uses the read-only YouTube Data API scope (youtube.readonly); with Instagram, the Instagram API with Instagram Login; with TikTok, TikTok Login Kit.
  • What we store: those numbers and the sign-in tokens, kept in private Cloudflare storage used only by this dashboard. We never see or store your passwords.
  • How it is used: only to show your own stats back to you on the dashboard. Nothing is posted, edited or deleted on any platform.
  • Sharing: nothing is sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising.
  • Google user data: Content HQ's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Deleting: choose Disconnect in the dashboard's account menu, or remove Content HQ from your account's app permissions (TikTok settings, Google account permissions, or Instagram's Apps and websites). Revoked tokens stop all reads, and the stored data for that account can be deleted on request.
